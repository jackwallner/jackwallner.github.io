Health & body

  • Total Calories - Daily Tracker: Private calorie and step tracking from HealthKit, with TDEE burn on Watch and widgets.
  • Desk Reset: Posture Practice: A three-minute guided posture practice with live AirPods cues and optional all-day monitoring.
  • Migraine Headache Tracker Log: One-tap headache logging that pairs each entry with health and barometric weather context.
  • Fitness Habit Tracker: Auto: Automatic fitness streak discovery from Apple Health.
  • Simple GLP: GLP-1 Dose Tracker: One-tap GLP-1 dose logging for weekly shots and daily pills, with schedules and optional Health context.
  • VO2 Max & Cardio Fitness: A focused view of Apple Health cardio fitness estimates, fitness age, trends, and targets.
  • Trip Comfort: Motion Sickness: Four drug-free comfort routines for journeys, with breathing guidance and paced wrist pulses.
  • Protein Tracker - Grams Today: One number on your wrist: the grams of protein you have tracked today. No calorie counting, no AI food scanning.
  • Caffeine Tracker: Bedtime: Preview what another coffee may leave at bedtime before you log it, on iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Daylight: Minutes Outside: Daylight time from Apple Health against a computed sunset, so you know the latest you can head out and still hit your target.
